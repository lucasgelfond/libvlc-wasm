# Sample credits

The player's one-click samples, where each comes from, and its licence. Every
file here is generated by a script in `apps/player/scripts/` (the Blu-ray by
`tests/make-bluray.sh`), so it can be rebuilt from its sources.

| File | What it is | Source | Licence | Length |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `showcase.iso` | "Tomorrow Vision", a DVD-Video image with motion menus: MPEG-2 + AC-3, two audio tracks, English and Spanish subtitles, a credits title. `make-showcase-dvd.sh` / `.py` | Footage: *Design for Dreaming* (1956, General Motors / MPO Productions), https://archive.org/details/Designfo1956, and *Living Stereo* (1958, RCA Victor / Jam Handy), https://archive.org/details/LivingSt1958, both from the Prelinger Archives. Menus, chrome type, sphere, synth music, subtitles and credits: generated for this disc. Type: DejaVu fonts. | Footage: public domain (Prelinger Archives). Everything we generated: CC0. DejaVu fonts: Bitstream Vera / DejaVu licence. | Feature 48 s, credits 30 s, two 12 s menu loops |
| `bluray.iso` | An unencrypted Blu-ray image (BDMV, H.264 + AC-3, three chapters). `tests/make-bluray.sh` | *Big Buck Bunny*, (c) copyright 2008, Blender Foundation, www.bigbuckbunny.org; https://download.blender.org/peach/bigbuckbunny_movies/ | CC BY 3.0 | 60 s |
| `sintel-psx.str` | A PlayStation STR cutscene: MDEC (BS v2) 320x240 15 fps + XA-ADPCM 37.8 kHz stereo, 2352-byte sectors, encoded with psxavenc. `make-str.sh` | The *Sintel* trailer, (c) copyright Blender Foundation, durian.blender.org; https://download.blender.org/durian/trailer/sintel_trailer-480p.mp4 (11.5 s to 40 s) | CC BY 3.0 | 28 s |
| `elephants-dream.wmv` | Windows Media: WMV2 (Windows Media Video 8) + WMA2 in ASF. `make-wmv.sh` | *Elephants Dream*, (c) copyright 2006, Blender Foundation / Netherlands Media Art Institute, www.elephantsdream.org; https://download.blender.org/ED/elephantsdream-480-h264-st-aac.mov (7:00 to 7:30) | CC BY 2.5 | 30 s |
| `wasm-64.sid` | "Wasm 64", Commodore 64 music: a PSID file with its own 6502 player (bass and drums, filtered pulse arpeggios, a lead with vibrato). `make-sid.mjs` | Composed and programmed for libvlc-wasm | CC0 | 54 s, then loops |
| `wasm-drive.vgm` | "Wasm Drive", Sega Genesis / Mega Drive music: YM2612 FM + SN76489 PSG register writes. `make-vgm.mjs` | Composed and programmed for libvlc-wasm | CC0 | 60 s (loops from 15 s) |

Not gallery samples: `everyday/` holds the formats page's generated files, and
`cenc-clearkey.mp4` is a generated ClearKey test file.
