# Sample credits

The player's one-click samples, where each comes from, and its licence. Every
file here is generated by a script in `apps/player/scripts/` (the Blu-ray by
`tests/make-bluray.sh`), so it can be rebuilt from its sources.

| File | What it is | Source | Licence | Length |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `design-for-dreaming.iso` | "Design for Dreaming and other films" (from the Prelinger Archives): a DVD-Video image with motion menus, scene selection, three chapters, English and Spanish subtitles (notes), a bonus film and credits. MPEG-2 + AC-3. `make-dvd.sh` | *Design for Dreaming* (1956), MPO Productions for General Motors, https://archive.org/details/Designfo1956; *Living Stereo* (1958), the Jam Handy Organization for RCA Victor, https://archive.org/details/LivingSt1958. All pictures and sound on the disc are the films' own. | Both films: public domain (Prelinger Archives; each item page carries the Public Domain mark). Menus, subtitles and authoring: CC0. Type: Berkshire Swash and Jost, SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`scripts/fonts/`). | Feature 1:30, bonus 0:20, credits 0:20, two 12 s menu loops |
| `bluray.iso` | An unencrypted Blu-ray image (BDMV, H.264 + AC-3, three chapters). `tests/make-bluray.sh` | *Big Buck Bunny*, (c) copyright 2008, Blender Foundation, www.bigbuckbunny.org; https://download.blender.org/peach/bigbuckbunny_movies/ | CC BY 3.0 | 1:00 |
| `a-is-for-atom.str` | A PlayStation STR cutscene: MDEC (BS v2) 320x240 15 fps + XA-ADPCM 37.8 kHz stereo, 2352-byte sectors, encoded with psxavenc. `make-str.sh` | *A Is for Atom* (1953), John Sutherland Productions for General Electric, https://archive.org/details/isforAto1953 (7:00 to 7:30) | Public domain (Prelinger Archives; Public Domain mark on the item page) | 0:30 |
| `story-of-television.wmv` | Windows Media: WMV2 (Windows Media Video 8) 320x240 + WMA2 in ASF. `make-wmv.sh` | *The Story of Television* (1956), William J. | `wasm-64.sid` | "Wasm 64", Commodore 64 music: a PSID file with its own 6502 player (bass and drums, filtered pulse arpeggios, a lead with vibrato). `make-sid.mjs` | Composed and programmed for libvlc-wasm | CC0 | 0:54, then loops |
| `wasm-drive.vgm` | "Wasm Drive", Sega Genesis / Mega Drive music: YM2612 FM + SN76489 PSG register writes. `make-vgm.mjs` | Composed and programmed for libvlc-wasm | CC0 | 1:00 (loops from 0:15) |

Not gallery samples: `everyday/` holds the formats page's generated files, and
`cenc-clearkey.mp4` is a generated ClearKey test file.

The Prelinger items' public-domain status is as the Internet Archive records
it: each item's `licenseurl` is `http://creativecommons.org/licenses/publicdomain/`,
shown on its page as the Public Domain mark. Licences: CC BY 3.0
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/, CC0
