<vlc-player>

A drop-in for <video>: same attributes, methods and events, but it plays what VLC plays. The whole integration is the markup below plus one import.

<script type="module">import '@libvlc-wasm/core/element';</script>
<vlc-player src="trailer.rm" controls autoplay></vlc-player>

player.addEventListener('timeupdate', () => console.log(player.currentTime));
player.player.selectTrack(...)   // the full libvlc-wasm Player, when you need more
